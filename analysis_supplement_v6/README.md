This is a post-hoc analysis of existing outputs. No new inference, harness execution
or human annotation is included. Historical and pilot inputs/verdicts remain unchanged.

    python tools/analyze_control_pairs.py --output /path/to/new-output-directory

On the ten unresolved follow-up tasks, candidate/developer acceptance is 3/10 versus
3/10 for both the test-blind maintainer and Cihan adapters, and 4/10 versus 3/10 for
Shi draft. Discordant counts are (1,1), (1,1) and (2,1); exploratory two-sided exact
McNemar p-values are all 1.0. Small samples provide no evidence of control separation
and are not evidence of equivalence. The adapters differ from the historical
instruments; Shi draft additionally sees developer references.

The two hosted-rationale examples are the first non-accepted agent cases in lexical
instance-ID order among 30 non-accepted cases, without selection for correctness.
They are descriptive model claims, not adjudicated defects. A reference to the
merged fix is a possible recognition cue, not proof of training contamination.
